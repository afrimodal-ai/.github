## AfriModal AI

Building and evaluating speech, language, and multimodal AI for African languages.

AfriModal AI is an independent research programme led by [Kayode Olaleye](https://github.com/kayodeolaleye). The work centres on evaluation and reliability of speech-capable and multimodal language models, low-resource and extremely low-resource languages, cross-lingual transfer, and the datasets and tokenization that the rest depends on. Applications such as healthcare communication are used as testbeds.

Code, benchmark specifications and reproducible experiments are released here as the work is ready. Datasets and models go to Hugging Face; papers to arXiv and peer-reviewed venues.

Website: <https://afrimodal-ai.github.io/>
